Questions

How do I cancel?

In the app, open your account menu, then Your plan, then Cancel renewal. You keep it until the paid period ends.

Will you remind me before it renews?

Yes. We email you a few days before each renewal with the date and the price.

Who gets the student price?

Students, and graduates for 2 years after graduating. Confirm a school email once in the app while still studying.

What does every 3 months include?

Three months at once: 150 tailored applications and 9 live interviews, for less than three monthly payments.

What happens when a plan ends?

You go back to Free. Your resumes and applications stay on your computer.

Where do I buy a plan?

Inside the app. Stripe takes the payment, so your card details never reach us.

Is there a version with no limits?

Yes. The community edition is free and unlimited, run from source with your own AI key.
