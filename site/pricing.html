Questions

What happens when a pass ends?

You go back to Free. Your jobs, resumes, applications and interview history are still on your computer, because they were never anywhere else. Nothing is charged, and there is nothing to cancel.

Can I buy a pass while one is running?

Yes. The days and the live interviews add to what you have left, so you never lose time you paid for.

Where do I buy a pass?

Inside the app, from your account. Payment goes through Stripe, and your card details never reach us. The website does not sell anything.

Why is there a free community edition at all?

Because the people who need this most, students on F-1 and OPT, often cannot legally earn money yet. The code is AGPL-3.0 on GitHub. Run it from source with your own AI key and the whole engine is yours, including unlimited tailoring.

A pass pays for the things that cost us money per use: the bundled AI and the live interviewer.

What do the interview packs cost?

One live interview for $9, three for $24, five for $39. They are sold while a pass is active, and they never expire.

Do you store my resume or my applications?

No. They stay in a folder on your machine. On a pass, text is sent to the AI through our broker to be written, then discarded. We keep a count of what was used for billing, and nothing else. The privacy policy lists every service involved.
