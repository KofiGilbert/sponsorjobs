Questions

What happens when a pass ends?

You go back to Free. Your resumes and applications stay on your computer. Nothing is charged.

Where do I buy a pass?

Inside the app. Stripe takes the payment, so your card details never reach us. A second pass adds to the first.

Is there a version with no limits?

Yes. The community edition is free and unlimited, run from source with your own AI key.
