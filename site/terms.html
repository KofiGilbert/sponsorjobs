What SponsorJobs is

A tool that helps you find jobs, tailor your resume and practise interviews. Sponsorship marks come from public government records about employers. They are not a promise that a role sponsors visas or that you will be hired. Live interviews are practice simulations, not real interviews. Nothing in the app or on this website is immigration or legal advice.

Your content

Your resume and answers are yours. You are responsible for what you submit to employers, and for making sure it is true. Where the app fills or sends an application for you, it does so from material you reviewed, and you remain the applicant.

Applying on your behalf

When you use Auto-apply, SponsorJobs may do these things for you:

  • Accept the privacy notices and terms a job site requires before it takes your application.
  • Create an account on a job site that needs one, with your SponsorJobs email. The password stays on your computer.
  • Fill the site's forms with the answers you saved.

It never accepts marketing or cookie choices for you, and it never solves a captcha. It never submits an application without your Send, except on sites we have checked allow it and only while you have Autonomous submit turned on. Every notice it accepted is listed on the application before you send it.

You can turn each of these off in the app: open My profile and find What I may do for you. When one is off and a job needs it, that job waits for you.

Passes and payments

Passes are one payment for a fixed period and do not renew. Extra interview packs never expire. Prices are shown before you pay, and payment is taken by Stripe. If something goes wrong with a purchase, email us and we will put it right or refund you.

Refunds

A pass or interview pack you have not used yet is refunded in full if you ask within 14 days of buying it. Once you have used it, we refund the part you did not get if the service failed on our side, for example an interview that did not run. To ask, email us from the address on your account with the receipt date; we reply within five working days, and Stripe returns the money to your card within ten. Nothing is sold on a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel.

Fair use

Do not misuse the service, try to break its limits, resell access, or use it to apply to jobs in ways a job site does not allow. The app will not automate a LinkedIn account or get around a site's bot protection, and neither may you through it.

No guarantees

The service is provided as it is. We do our best to keep it accurate and available, but we cannot guarantee job outcomes or that every record is current. Our liability is limited to what you paid us in the last 12 months.
