What it does

Finds jobs at employers that sponsor

Pulls postings from official public job boards. Each employer is tagged with its visa record from public US government data: H-1B, green card (PERM), E-Verify for STEM OPT, and cap-exempt status.

Flags "no sponsorship" postings

Postings that say they will not sponsor are marked before you spend time on them.

Tailors your résumé to each job, on one page

Rewords your real experience toward the job's own language, keeps the page from overflowing, and shows which of the job's keywords you cover and which you miss. It never adds a skill to a role where you did not use it.

Drafts the cover letter

And answers to screening questions, written from your own history, for you to edit.

Interview practice

A recorded, scored first-round screen, then a simulated interviewer that asks questions from the actual job description.

Assisted apply

Fills application forms for you to review. You click submit. It never automates LinkedIn and never tries to get around a site's bot protection.

Demo video

A 60 to 90 second screen recording goes here: find a job, tailor the résumé, review the result.

Your data

  • Your data stays on your computer. Your profile, résumés, and applications are stored locally. SponsorJobs has no account system and no server that holds them.
  • Text goes to the AI provider you choose, with your own key. To write, SponsorJobs sends the relevant text to Anthropic or OpenAI using an API key you create. You pay the provider directly for what you use. SponsorJobs stores none of it.
  • The shared job list contains no personal data. It is a list of public job postings plus public government sponsorship records. Your profile is never sent to it.

How to install

Two things first. SponsorJobs typesets your résumé with LaTeX, so LaTeX must be installed. And it needs an AI key from Anthropic (recommended) or OpenAI; the first-run setup asks for it and keeps it on your computer.

macOS (Apple Silicon)

  1. Install LaTeX. In Terminal: brew install --cask mactex-no-gui
  2. Download the .dmg, open it, and drag SponsorJobs to Applications.
  3. On first open, macOS will say it cannot verify the developer. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Once.

The app is not yet signed with an Apple Developer certificate, which is why macOS asks.

Windows

  1. Install LaTeX: MiKTeX. Let it install missing packages on the fly.
  2. Download SponsorJobs-Setup.exe and run it.
  3. Windows SmartScreen may warn that the publisher is unknown. Click More info, then Run anyway.

The installer is not yet code-signed, which is why SmartScreen asks.

Linux, Intel Macs, or just prefer source: see the README.
