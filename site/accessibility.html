What we aim for

We want SponsorJobs, this website and the desktop app, to work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard alone, magnification, or who have asked their system for less motion. The standard we build to is WCAG 2.1 at level AA.

What works today

  • Every control on this site can be reached and used with the keyboard, and the focus is always visible.
  • Headings, landmarks and labels are in place for screen readers. Images that carry meaning have text; decorative ones are skipped.
  • Text and background meet the AA contrast ratio in both light and dark mode, and the site follows your system's choice.
  • Animations and the app clips stop when your system asks for reduced motion.
  • The page scales to 200 percent zoom and to phone widths without sideways scrolling.

Known limits

  • The practice interviews use a camera and a microphone. The recorded round works with the microphone alone; the live round needs both.
  • Job postings and employer pages are shown as the employer published them, and we cannot fix their accessibility.

Tell us

If any part of SponsorJobs is hard to use, email support@sponsorjobs.ai with the page or screen and what happened. We answer within five working days and fix what we can in the next release.
