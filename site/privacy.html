Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026. SponsorJobs is made by BlackOrigin, Inc. Contact: kofig@blackorigin.org.

The short version

Your resume, job history, applications and interview recordings stay on your own computer. We do not store them, sell them or use them to train AI models.

What we hold on our servers

  • Your account: an account id and, if you sign in, your email address.
  • Usage counts: how many tailored resumes and live interviews your account has used, so plan limits work.
  • Purchases: which pass or interview pack you bought and when. Card details go to Stripe and never reach us.

Services that process data for us

  • Anthropic writes tailored text. Only the text needed for that request is sent, and it is not used to train models.
  • Tavus runs live practice interviews. Video and audio stream between you and Tavus; we receive only the transcript to score it, and it is stored on your computer.
  • Stripe takes payments and emails receipts.
  • Resend emails your sign-in codes.
  • Google, if you choose "Continue with Google", tells us your email address. We ask for nothing else.
  • Cloudflare hosts our server, website and job feed.

The browser extension

The extension reads the job page you are looking at to show sponsorship facts. Company names are looked up inside your browser and are not sent to us.

Your choices

You can use SponsorJobs without signing in. To delete your account, email us and we will remove your email address and usage records within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your computer.

Children

SponsorJobs is not intended for anyone under 16.
